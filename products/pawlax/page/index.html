Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pawlax need to be frozen, refrigerated, or plugged in to work?

No! Pawlax uses advanced cooling fabric that activates naturally from body heat — simply lay it down and it starts working within seconds. No freezing, charging, or electricity required.

Is Pawlax safe for both cats and dogs?

Yes. Pawlax is made from non-toxic, pet-safe materials suitable for cats and dogs of all sizes and breeds, so the whole household can share the comfort.

Will Pawlax protect my furniture or car seats from spills and accidents?

Absolutely. The base is 100% waterproof, creating a protective barrier against drool, spills, and pet accidents so your furniture, floors, and car interiors stay clean and dry.

How do I clean Pawlax?

Pawlax's cover is fully machine washable. Simply toss it in on a gentle cycle and air dry to keep it fresh and ready for your pet's next nap.
