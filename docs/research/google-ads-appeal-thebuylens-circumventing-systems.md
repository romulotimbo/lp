# Google Ads appeal — Circumventing systems (TheBuyLens)

Paste-ready statement. English. Destinations live as of 8 September 2026.

---

Hello Google Ads Review Team,

I am appealing the Circumventing systems decision on this advertiser and on the `thebuylens.com` destinations. I believe the review treated an independent affiliate review as an attempt to hide a different offer from AdsBot. That was not our intent, and the live pages have been corrected so that reviewers and visitors see the same commercial identity, the same content, and the same destination.

## What these pages are

TheBuyLens publishes independent product reviews. We are not the manufacturer.

- https://burntide.thebuylens.com — independent review of Burntide dietary-supplement gummies. Outbound buttons open the official store at burntide.us (affiliate link, disclosed).
- https://advanced-amino.thebuylens.com — independent review of Advanced Amino Formula. Outbound buttons open the official Advanced Bionutritionals page (affiliate link, disclosed).

Both hosts are the same registered domain. We did not create a new domain or a new Ads account to republish a disapproved destination. We kept the existing URLs and changed the pages.

Google tag `AW-18351905109` is installed on both pages as a standard gtag config / page view. Review outbound clicks are not treated as a checkout conversion.

## Why this is not cloaking

We do not show AdsBot one page and visitors another.

- `robots.txt` allows all user agents, including AdsBot (`User-agent: *` / `Allow: /`).
- There is no user-agent, geo, or device branch that swaps the offer.
- There is no full-page interstitial, injected checkout overlay, or inescapable popup on these review URLs.
- The HTML, copy, and outbound hrefs are the same for crawlers and people. Buttons open the official merchant site in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow sponsored"`.
- We are not using a click tracker to send users to a different, policy-violating site while showing Google a clean review.

If the earlier version looked like a “scam alert” or a first-person health story that then sent the click to a merchant checkout, that was a clarity problem on our side — not a second hidden site. We have removed that pattern.

## Changes made so the review process can see the real page

These edits are live on both destinations.

**Identity and affiliation (Misleading representation / undisclosed affiliate)**

- The first viewport now states that TheBuyLens publishes the page and that we are not the manufacturer.
- The affiliate disclosure is in the hero, not only the footer. It says that buttons open the official merchant site and that we may earn a commission.
- Footer branding is “TheBuyLens · independent review”, not the product company name.

**Destination (Circumventing systems — cloaking / hidden hop)**

- Button labels are descriptive: “Visit the official Burntide website” and “Visit the official Advanced Bionutritionals page”.
- Microcopy names the destination domain. We no longer use “Claim the official offer” or “disclosure is in the footer”.
- The official URL is the only outbound sales destination. There is no rotating URL and no cloaked redirect.

**Clickbait and health claims (Clickbait ads / Healthcare / Unreliable claims)**

- Burntide: removed “legit fat burner or a cheap scam”, “2026 alert”, “Don’t buy until…”, and “fat burner” / blood-sugar language as our own claims.
- Amino: removed the H1 “for age-related muscle loss”. The headline now describes the page (“what’s on the official page”).
- Manufacturer health statements are dated citations of their letter (for example Burntide’s “FDA-registered facility” wording is explained as factory language, not FDA approval of the product; Amino’s 30% muscle-mass figure is labeled as the company’s number).
- We do not claim the supplements diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. FDA supplement disclaimers remain in the footer.

**Social proof**

- Amino reader notes are labeled as composite illustrative notes, not verified customer reviews.
- Burntide does not republish named testimonials from the official sales letter.

## What we are asking

Please re-review the live URLs above. They are affiliate reviews with original copy, a visible disclosure, a single declared merchant destination, and the same content for Google and users. We are not trying to bypass the review process, recreate a disapproved page on a new domain, or show AdsBot a different site than visitors see.

Thank you for reconsidering the decision.
